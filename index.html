Enhanced Floor Plan Designer Instructions

  • Select/Move Tool: Click to select items, drag to move them.
  • Draw Wall Tool: Click and drag to draw straight wall segments.
  • Rotate Tool: Select an item and use the rotate handle or press 'R' to rotate.
  • Place Item Tool: Click on the canvas to place the selected item.
  • Items Palette: Use the category tabs to browse different item types. Drag items from the palette onto the canvas.
  • Grid Toggle: Show or hide the grid for precise placement.
  • Snap to Grid: Enable/disable snapping items to the grid.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts:
    • Delete/Backspace: Remove selected item
    • R: Rotate selected item
    • Ctrl+Z: Undo
    • Ctrl+Y: Redo
  • Save/Load: Save your design to browser storage or load a previous one.
  • Export: Export your design as JSON data or as a PNG image.
